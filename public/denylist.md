## Criteria

- **gambling** — Online casinos, sportsbooks, betting bonuses, slots, crash/colour-prediction gambling apps, and casino affiliate listings.
- **fake-engagement** — Sells followers, likes, views, or subscribers (SMM panels and similar).
- **fake-reviews** — Sells reviews (for example Google reviews).
- **fake-traffic** — Sells website traffic.
- **link-spam** — Sells backlinks, black-hat SEO forums, essay/homework mills used for link spam, and link-building agencies that buy sponsorships for backlinks.
- **piracy** — Piracy guides, Pirate Bay proxies, Fire TV Stick sideloading / IPTV guides.
- **adult** — Adult directories.
